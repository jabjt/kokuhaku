# Changelog

Kokuhaku runs on Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric), launched through Prism Launcher.
Versions before 1.3.0 weren't tagged at the time; their entries were written up afterwards.

## 1.3.0 — 5 November 2026

### New
- **Watch a solo run.** If one person is playing and anyone else picks Spectator, the spectators see the whole run through that player's eyes. With two or more players, spectators are still ghosts.

### Fixed
- Sitting down and sneaking at the same moment could drop you through the floor.
- Getting up from a chair could put you through a wall, in a different room. You now stand up where you were standing when you sat down.
- Walking to school in multiplayer: both players got the same companions, who were dragged along two routes at once. That made them move too fast and walk through walls. Each companion now walks with one player only.
- The forest's scares now trigger reliably among tall trees.

## 1.2.1 — 4 November 2026

### Fixed
- The Mac app couldn't find Prism Launcher unless it was in one exact place. It now searches the Mac for it, and asks you to point at it if it still can't.

## 1.2.0 — 3 November 2026

### New
- **New launcher** for Windows and Mac: a dark window with its own title bar, a Launch button and a sidebar of versions (upcoming ones marked WIP). Prism Launcher stays hidden while the game is set up and started.
- **Mac version.**
- **Map edited:** mountains north of the town, joined to it by foothills. They're downloaded on first launch.
- **Mt. Chinetsu ski run** down the real mountain, with snow on the high slopes.
- **The forest.** Something happens in the trees behind the mountain.
- Trees on the foothills, so they run on into the forest.

### Changed
- **Songs edited:** new radio playlist.

## 1.1.0 — October 2026

### Changed
- A run is now **14 days** instead of 30, with the story, schedule and clues rescaled to fit.
- People walk the way people do: fitted routes through town, round corners and through doors, no hopping up steps.
- The game starts fullscreen.

### New
- The radio, styled like a phone music app.
- Rain, school life, changes at home, and lunch.

### Fixed
- A seated girl's head shook violently during scenes.
- Furniture stairs going the wrong way.

## 1.0.0

- The first version: find out which girl is the yandere before she finds you.
